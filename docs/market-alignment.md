# German AI job-market alignment — September 2026

This project was shaped around recurring requirements visible in current German AI engineering roles: production-grade Python/FastAPI, LangChain/LangGraph, RAG, vector databases, structured outputs, evaluation, observability, Docker/CI/CD, React/TypeScript and human-in-the-loop governance.

Selected public examples researched during design:
- Arcode Systems — AI Engineer / Agentic AI Engineer: LangChain/LangGraph, MCP, RAG, pgvector/Qdrant, FastAPI, agent evals, Langfuse/Phoenix/RAGAS, CI/CD and observability.
- HTGF — AI Engineer: LangGraph agent pipelines, FastAPI/Pydantic, MCP servers, vector/graph retrieval, Langfuse, React/TypeScript and Docker.
- Xcubator — Senior AI Engineer: stateful LangGraph, RAG, human-in-the-loop, FastAPI, pgvector/Qdrant, audit logging and MCP.
- freenet — Senior AI Platform Engineer: LangGraph/LangChain, FastAPI, Next.js/React, PostgreSQL and operating LLM systems with SLOs/incident handling.

The goal is not to claim production experience from a portfolio project. It is to provide inspectable evidence that the architecture, testing and operational concepts have been implemented end to end.
